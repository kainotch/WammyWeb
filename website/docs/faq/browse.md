# Browse FAQ

Everything you need to know about browsing sources, discovering new titles, and organizing catalogs in Wammy.

---

## How is the Browse tab organized?

The Browse tab contains 6 logically ordered sub-tabs to separate native content from external plugins:
1. **Manga Sources**: Native built-in manga sources displayed in a quick-access grid followed by pinned and installed sources.
2. **Novel Sources**: Native built-in light novel and web novel sources.
3. **Manga Extensions**: Repository browser for external Mihon/Tachiyomi APK extensions.
4. **Novel Extensions**: Repository browser for external LNReader JavaScript novel plugins.
5. **Manga Migration**: Fast source-to-source migration tool for transferring manga series between sources.
6. **Novel Migration**: Source-to-source migration tool for light novels.

---

## What do the Star Ratings and View Counts on cards mean?

Wammy parses live metadata directly from supported source websites:
- **Star Rating Pill**: Shows the community score (e.g. `4.8`) parsed from the source's catalog.
- **Eye Icon / View Count**: Indicates the total view count (e.g. `1.2M`, `450K`) recorded by the upstream platform.

These indicators appear directly on series cards across the Discover feed, Browse catalogs, search results, and your personal library.

---

## How does Global Search work?

Tap the **Travel Explore** (globe) icon in the top toolbar of the Browse tab to open **Global Search**:
- Type a query to search across all your enabled built-in sources and installed extensions simultaneously.
- Results appear in real time with individual source cards, allowing you to compare translation groups, chapter counts, and cover art.

---

## How do I pin favorite sources or create custom pin groups?

- **Quick Pin**: Tap the pin icon on any source row to pin it directly to the top of your sources list.
- **Custom Pin Groups**: Long-press any pinned source to create named groups (e.g. "Manhwa Only", "Translations", "Daily Reads") and organize your sources cleanly.

---

## What does the "18+" toggle button in the top bar do?

Tapping the **18+** button in the top bar toggles the display of adult / mature content sources:
- When turned **Off**: Mature and 18+ sources are hidden from the catalog list and global search.
- When turned **On**: Adult sources become visible in the catalog for exploration.
- You can further customize mature content visibility in **Settings > Browse > Show adult sources**.
