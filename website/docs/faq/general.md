# General FAQ

## What is Wammy?
Wammy is a free, open-source manga and light novel reader for Android. It uses built-in sources instead of extensions.

## Is Wammy a fork of Mihon?
Yes, it builds upon Mihon but diverges by focusing on built-in sources and combined Manga/Novel feeds.

## Is it free?
Yes. No ads, no tracking, completely free.
