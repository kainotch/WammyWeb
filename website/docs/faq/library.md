# Library FAQ

Learn how to manage, customize, and update your personal collection of manga and light novels in Wammy.

---

## How do I switch between Manga and Novels in the Library?

By default, Wammy separates your collection into dedicated bottom dock tabs for **Novels** and **Manga (Library)**.

If you prefer to see both manga and novels unified inside a single library interface, navigate to:
**Settings > Library > Joined Library** and toggle the switch. Wammy will combine them into one seamless view with category tabs across the top.

---

## How do I check for new chapters?

- **Manual Update**: Pull down from the top of your Library screen to trigger an immediate update check across all tracked series.
- **Automated Background Updates**: Configure scheduled automatic checks in **Settings > Library > Automated library updates**. You can choose intervals (e.g. every 6 hours, 12 hours, or daily) and restrict updates to Wi-Fi only or when your device is charging.

---

## What display modes and grid layouts are available?

Tap the three-dot menu in the Library toolbar and select **Display Mode** to customize your grid layout:
- **Compact Grid**: Maximizes screen space, showing dense cover cards with titles below.
- **Comfortable Grid**: Adds extra breathing room, displaying unread chapter counts, download status pills, and larger cover thumbnails.
- **List View**: Displays series in horizontal rows with comprehensive metadata, latest chapter title, and category assignments.
- **Grid Size**: Adjust the number of columns per row individually for portrait and landscape orientations.

---

## How do I batch-edit or reorganize multiple series?

Long-press any series card in your Library to enter **Multi-Selection Mode**:
- Tap additional cards to select them, or tap **Select All**.
- Use the bottom action bar to:
  - Move selected series into one or more categories.
  - Download all unread chapters in bulk.
  - Mark chapters as read or unread.
  - Delete downloaded files to free up internal storage.
  - Remove series completely from your library.

---

## How do unread badges and download indicators work?

- **Unread Badge**: A small pill badge on the corner of the cover showing how many unread chapters remain.
- **Download Badge**: Indicates how many chapters are saved offline on your device storage for reading without an internet connection.
- You can toggle badge visibility in **Settings > Library > Badges**.
