# Reader FAQ

Explore the reading engines for visual comics and text-based light novels in Wammy.

---

## What reading modes are available for Manga and Webtoons?

Wammy provides specialized viewing modes tailored for every comic style:
- **Webtoon (Continuous Vertical)**: Continuous, gapless vertical strip designed for Korean Webtoons and modern scrolling manhwa.
- **Right-to-Left (Traditional Manga)**: Standard Japanese manga reading order with smooth horizontal page flips.
- **Left-to-Right**: Reading order for Western comics and standard text flow.
- **Vertical (Paged)**: Vertical scrolling with page-by-page snapping.
- **Dual-Page Spread**: Automatically splits wide double-page spreads on phones or joins single pages side-by-side on tablets and foldable devices.

---

## How does the Light Novel Reader work?

Wammy includes a dedicated, native typography engine for light novels and web novels:
- **Custom Typography**: Choose from system fonts or load your own custom `.ttf` / `.otf` fonts. Adjust font scale, line spacing, letter spacing, and paragraph indentation.
- **Reading Themes**: Select from preset themes including Pure Black (OLED battery saver), Sepia (easy on the eyes in warm lighting), Slate, and Classic Light.
- **Chapter Navigation**: Access the full chapter list from the side drawer without leaving your reading position.

---

## Does the Novel Reader support Text-to-Speech (TTS)?

**Yes.** Wammy includes a built-in Text-to-Speech engine for novels:
- Tap the **Headphones / Audio** icon in the novel reader top bar to activate TTS narration.
- Customize reading speed, pitch, and voice engine through your device's Text-to-Speech settings.
- Features continuous playback across chapters, auto-scrolling synchronization, and background lockscreen audio controls.

---

## Can I use volume buttons to turn pages?

**Yes.** You can enable hardware volume key navigation for hands-free reading:
- Navigate to **Settings > Reader > Navigation**.
- Toggle **Volume keys** to turn pages.
- You can optionally invert the volume button direction (e.g. Volume Up for Next Page or Previous Page).

---

## How do tap zones work?

During reading, the screen is divided into configurable tap zones:
- Tapping the **Center** toggles the toolbar, chapter drawer, and reader controls.
- Tapping the **Right** or **Left** edges turns to the next or previous page.
- You can customize tap zone layouts and sensitivity in **Settings > Reader > Navigation > Tap zones**.
