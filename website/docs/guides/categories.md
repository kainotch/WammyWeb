# Library Categories

Organize your personal reading library with custom tabs, categories, and automated sorting rules.

---

## What are Categories?

Categories allow you to group your manga and light novels into custom tabs across the top of your Library screen. Common examples include:
- *Reading Now*
- *Completed*
- *Plan to Read*
- *Favorites*
- *On Hold*
- *Dropped*

---

## Creating and Managing Categories

1. Open **More > Settings > Library > Categories**.
2. Tap the **+** (Add) button in the bottom right corner.
3. Enter a name for your new category and tap **Create**.
4. To rename a category, tap its name in the list.
5. To delete a category, tap the trash can icon next to its name. *(Note: Deleting a category does not delete the manga inside it; they will simply return to the default category).*

---

## Reordering Category Tabs

The order of categories in **Settings > Categories** directly controls the tab order in your Library screen:
1. Tap and hold the drag handle (hamburger icon) next to any category.
2. Drag the category up or down to your desired position.
3. Return to your Library to see the updated tab order immediately.

---

## Assigning Titles to Categories

### For a Single Title:
1. Open the series details page.
2. Tap the **Edit Categories** button (or long-press the bookmark icon).
3. Check the box for each category you want the title to belong to. A series can belong to multiple categories simultaneously.

### For Multiple Titles (Batch Edit):
1. In your Library, long-press a series card to enter selection mode.
2. Tap all the series you want to reorganize.
3. Tap the **Folder / Category** icon in the bottom action bar.
4. Select the target category and tap **Save**.

---

## Setting a Default Category

If you want newly added series to automatically be placed into a specific category:
1. Go to **Settings > Library > Categories**.
2. Tap on your preferred category and toggle **Set as default category**.
