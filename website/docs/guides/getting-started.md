# Getting Started with Wammy

A comprehensive walkthrough to help you get the most out of Wammy on your Android device.

---

## 1. Navigating the Interface

Wammy uses a modern floating navigation dock with ergonomic one-handed navigation:
- **Discover**: Featured hero carousel showcasing trending titles, quick filter chips, and community news.
- **Novels**: Your personal library of saved light novels and web novels with unread chapter badges.
- **Manga (Library)**: Your personal library of saved comics, manhwa, and manga.
- **History**: Chronological reading history with instantaneous one-tap resume.
- **Browse**: Instant access to 20+ native built-in sources, external extension repositories, and source migration tools.
- **More**: Application settings, download queue, Google Drive cloud sync, user profiles, and about information.

---

## 2. Exploring Built-In Sources (Zero Setup Required)

You do not need to install extension APKs or add external repositories to start reading:
1. Tap the **Browse** icon in the bottom dock.
2. Under **Manga Sources**, you will find native scrapers for MangaDex, Webtoons, ComicK, Mangakakalot, Atsumaru, ManhuaPlus, WeebCentral, Aster Scans, and more.
3. Under **Novel Sources**, you will find direct access to FreeWebNovel, NovelFire, NovelBuddy, WuxiaBox, NovelCool, and others.
4. Tap any source to browse **Popular**, **Latest**, or filter by genre and tag.
5. Tap the **Global Search** (globe) icon in the top bar to search for a title across all sources simultaneously.

---

## 3. Adding Titles to Your Library

1. When you find a series you want to track, tap the series card to open its **Details** screen.
2. Tap the **Add to Library** button (heart icon).
3. Select which **Category** to place it in (e.g. "Reading", "Plan to Read", or "Favorites").
4. Once added, Wammy will automatically track new chapter releases and save your reading progress.

---

## 4. Reading Manga and Novels

- **Manga & Webtoons**: Tap any chapter to open the comic viewer. Tap the center of the screen to reveal the viewer settings in the bottom right corner, where you can toggle between **Webtoon** (continuous vertical strip), **Right-to-Left**, or **Left-to-Right** modes.
- **Light Novels**: Tap a novel chapter to open the native text reader. Tap the center to customize font size, typography, line height, background color themes, or tap the **Headphones** icon to activate **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** narration.

---

## 5. Setting Up Google Drive Cloud Sync

Keep your library, reading progress, and custom categories synchronized across all your devices:
1. Tap **More** in the bottom dock and select your **Profile / Cloud Sync**.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Wammy will automatically synchronize your library and reading status in the background directly to your private Google Drive AppData folder.
