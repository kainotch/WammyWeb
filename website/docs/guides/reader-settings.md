# Reader Configuration & Settings

Complete guide to configuring the comic viewer, webtoon engine, and light novel reader in Wammy.

---

## Comic & Manga Viewer Settings

Access comic settings while reading by tapping the center of your screen, then tapping the **Gear / Settings** icon in the bottom right corner.

### 1. Reading Modes
- **Webtoon (Continuous Vertical)**: Renders pages as a seamless, infinite vertical strip with zero padding between pages. Ideal for Korean webtoons and full-color manhwa.
- **Right-to-Left (Traditional Manga)**: Flips pages horizontally right-to-left matching original Japanese manga publication order.
- **Left-to-Right**: Standard Western comic reading order.
- **Vertical (Paged)**: Vertical page flips with snap-to-page behavior.

### 2. Scale Type
- **Fit Screen**: Automatically scales the page to fit both width and height within your display.
- **Fit Width**: Scales the page width to match your screen width; ideal for reading high-detail manga panels.
- **Fit Height**: Scales page height to display height.
- **Original Size**: Displays the image at 1:1 native resolution.

### 3. Display Enhancements
- **Crop Borders**: Automatically trims excess white or black margins from the edges of comic pages.
- **Color Filter**: Apply warm amber, grayscale, or invert colors for night reading.
- **32-Bit Color**: Enables high-fidelity 32-bit color rendering to eliminate banding on subtle gradients.

## Light Novel Reader Settings

Access novel settings by tapping the center of the screen while reading any light novel chapter.

### 1. Typography & Formatting
- **Font Size**: Scale text up or down dynamically from 12sp to 36sp.
- **Custom Font**: Choose from built-in typography or place your own `.ttf` / `.otf` font files in `Wammy/fonts` to read with custom book typefaces.
- **Line Spacing**: Adjust vertical spacing between text lines for comfortable long-form reading.
- **Text Alignment**: Switch between Justified, Left-aligned, or Center-aligned paragraphs.
- **Margins**: Set left and right screen padding to prevent text from touching device bezels.

### 2. Reading Themes
- **Pure Black (AMOLED)**: Deep pure black background (`#000000`) with soft white text to maximize battery life on OLED screens.
- **Sepia Warm**: Gentle warm tint reducing blue light for bedtime reading.
- **Slate**: Dark charcoal theme with reduced contrast for eye comfort.
- **Clean Light**: Crisp paper-white theme for bright daylight reading.

### 3. Text-to-Speech (TTS) Narration
- Tap the **Headphones** icon in the novel reader top bar to start hands-free voice narration.
- Control playback speed (0.5x to 3.0x) and voice pitch.
- Audio continues playing smoothly across chapter boundaries and when your device screen is locked.
