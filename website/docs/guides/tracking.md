# Tracking & Progress Synchronization

Connect your personal tracking accounts to synchronize your reading progress, chapter counts, and scores automatically.

---

## What is Tracker Integration?

Trackers automatically record which chapters you have read and sync that progress with online anime/manga databases. When you finish reading a chapter in Wammy, your linked account on AniList or MyAnimeList is updated instantly in the background.

---

## Supported Services

Wammy supports two-way synchronization with six major tracking platforms:
- **AniList**: Comprehensive tracking with score systems, custom lists, and re-read tracking.
- **MyAnimeList (MAL)**: Standard community database integration.
- **Kitsu**: Fast progress tracking and status synchronization.
- **Shikimori**: Russian-focused tracker integration.
- **Bangumi**: Chinese media database and progress logging.
- **MangaUpdates**: Direct chapter tracking with MangaUpdates records.

---

## How to Connect a Tracker

1. Navigate to **More > Settings > Tracking**.
2. Tap the service you want to connect (e.g. **AniList** or **MyAnimeList**).
3. Tap **Log in**. A secure browser window will open requesting authorization.
4. Sign in to your account and grant permission to Wammy.
5. Once authenticated, your account username will appear in the settings list.

---

## Linking a Series to a Tracker

1. Open any series from your Library or Browse catalogs to view its **Details** screen.
2. Scroll to the **Tracking** section at the bottom of the details page.
3. Tap **Track** on your connected service.
4. Wammy will automatically search the tracker database for matching titles.
5. Select the correct matching entry from the search results.
6. Choose your initial **Status** (e.g. *Reading*), **Score**, and confirm.

---

## Automatic Updates

Once a series is linked:
- Every time you finish reading a chapter, Wammy updates your progress on the tracker in the background.
- If you read chapters on other devices or mark them as completed on the tracker website, Wammy will synchronize that progress locally when refreshing your library.
