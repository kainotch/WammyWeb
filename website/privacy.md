# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2026

Wammy ("we", "us", or "our") is a free, open-source Android reader application for manga, manhwa, manhua, and light novels. We believe privacy is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy describes how Wammy handles information when you use our application.

---

## 1. Zero Tracking & No Data Selling

- **We do not track you:** Wammy does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or reading history.
- **Local-first design:** Your library, categories, reading progress, and downloaded chapters reside entirely on your local device.

---

## 2. Discord Integration

When you connect your Discord account in Wammy:
- **Authentication:** We use Discord OAuth2 with Proof Key for Code Exchange (PKCE).
- **Scopes Requested:** Wammy requests only the `identify` scope.
- **Data Accessed:** We only read your public Discord user ID, username, and avatar to identify your connected profile and display your account in Settings.
- **Rich Presence:** If enabled, Wammy displays your current reading activity (e.g. title and chapter) as your Discord status.
- **What We Do Not Access:** We cannot read your private messages, access your friends list, join servers on your behalf, or send messages as you.

---

## 3. Cloud Backups & Sync

- If you enable DropSauce, Google Drive, or self-hosted cloud sync, your library metadata and reading history are encrypted and transmitted directly between your device and your chosen cloud storage provider.
- We do not run intermediary servers that store or inspect your backups.

---

## 4. Diagnostics & Crash Reporting

- **Optional Telemetry:** Wammy includes optional, privacy-respecting crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) to help developers diagnose crashes and bugs.
- **Full User Control:** You can toggle Crashlytics and anonymous analytics on or off at any time under **Settings > Security and privacy**.
- Reports contain non-identifiable technical data such as device model, OS version, and stack traces.

---

## 5. Third-Party Sources

Wammy enables users to browse content from third-party online sources and repositories. When you browse or download chapters from external sources:
- Your requests go directly to those external websites.
- Those services have their own independent privacy policies and terms.
